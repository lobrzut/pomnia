## Anti-flat — style directives (feedback 2026-07-07)

The flat-ui refinements landed on a **generic flat corporate icon** (owner: "everything drifts into the Lory style"). Later iterations: **no** flat-ui, no soft corporate look.

### FORBID

`flat design, minimalist vector logo, corporate app icon, gradient squircle, generic tech logo, dribbble logo, behance flat icon, simple geometric mark, Lory style, flat-ui, material design icon, ios app icon template, soft, mild, generic, rounded friendly app icon, pastel, subtle, bland, soft glow, cute, game UI`

### SEEK

`dimensional depth, subtle emboss or engraving, organic texture moss bark amber resin, hand-crafted feel, Slavic folk art influence, carved wood or amber inlay, atmospheric rim lighting, tactile surface, symbolic object with depth — NOT illustration scene, NOT landscape`

**Bold pass:** additionally `sharp angular geometry, high contrast chiaroscuro, crystal facets, iron patina, cracked bark, ritual threshold, monumental artifact, aggressive folk-art angles`

## Three concept directions (plus one bonus)

### 1. Moss Vault

**Metaphor:** memory as a forest vault — a double mandorla (vesica) forming a labyrinth, with amber light at the centre like sunlight trapped in moss.

**Why it is original:**
- The vesica plus labyrinth is our own composition, not a copied kolovrat or Futhark
- Amber means "memory held" (a Slavic treasure), not a generic light bulb
- Concentric rings read better at 16×16 px (tray) than a letter does

**Depth description (anti-flat):** concentric rings carved into dark forest stone like a memory tunnel vault, volumetric amber glow from center like trapped sunlight in resin, moss texture on ring edges, embossed Slavic woven border (original, not Elder Futhark), carved wood / amber inlay feel, emerald–moss on void `#0a120e`, symbolic object only — no scene/landscape/text.

**Forbidden metaphors:** light bulb, letter R, kolovrat, swastika, the purple/cyan Reliqua palette, flat corporate icon.

**Assets:** `assets/generated/logo-concept-moss-vault.png` (shortlist) · refinements `logo-refine-moss-vault-v2/v3` · depth `logo-depth-moss-vault-v1/v2`.

---

### 2. Birch Thread

**Metaphor:** three tangled threads of memory — embroidery, birch bark, knots as in dream-bundles (without a literal dreamcatcher).

**Why it is original:**
- Drawn from embroidery rather than copying the kolovrat
- An abstract knot, not a letter "P"
- Silver-green birch is characteristic of Central Europe

**Historical note:** the first generation pulled magenta and teal — avoid those accents.

**Asset:** `assets/generated/logo-concept-birch-thread.png` — **REJECTED** (too controversial). Archive only.

---

### 3. Dew Sigil

**Metaphor:** morning dew on a geometric lattice — original runic geometry (not Elder Futhark), with a memory gate at the centre.

**Why it is original:**
- Our own grid of angles plus droplets, not a runic alphabet
- Dew means fresh memory, waking
- The vesica as a "dream gate"

**Depth description:** angular lattice sigil carved into dark stone / polished amber, three dew droplets as glass beads with rim light, recessed vesica memory gate, moss/bark micro-texture in grooves — symbolic carved object only, no landscape/trees/scene.

**Historical note:** v1 sometimes came out as a landscape rather than an icon — hold to one symbol and forbid landscape/scene/trees.

**Assets:** `assets/generated/logo-concept-dew-sigil.png` · refinements `logo-refine-dew-sigil-v2/v3` · hybrid `logo-refine-dew-vault-hybrid.png` · depth `logo-depth-dew-sigil-v1/v2`.

## Once a direction is chosen

1. The owner picks a concept (say **Moss Vault**)
2. Iterations happen outside this repo (private pipeline / manual editing) until the symbol is clean
3. Vector export (Figma / Inkscape) or manual vectorisation
4. `resources/icon.png` + `node scripts/gen-icon.mjs` → `resources/icon.ico` (if that script is still in the repo)
5. Landing page and UI: swap the violet/cyan accents for forest/moss/amber

**Temporarily (2026-07-09):** **bold-vault-v1** is in place as a placeholder — the final choice is still open.

**Do not do step 4 without an explicit "I pick X" from the owner** — other than a deliberate placeholder, as above.
