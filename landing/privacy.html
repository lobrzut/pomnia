Privacy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

The short version. If you join the waitlist we store one thing: your email address. We use it to tell you when Pomnia is released. There is no tracking on this site, no analytics, no advertising, and nothing is shared with anyone beyond the mailing provider that delivers the message. You can leave at any time, and leaving deletes your address.

Who is responsible

The controller of your data is Łukasz Obrzut, an individual developer based in Poland, operating the Pomnia project. You can reach me at hello@pomnia.ai about anything on this page.

What is collected, and when

Only if you submit the waitlist form: your email address, plus the date and time of your sign-up and confirmation. Those timestamps exist so I can demonstrate that you actually asked to be on the list.

If you just read the site: nothing. This page and the home page load no analytics, no advertising scripts, no social widgets, no web fonts and no third-party resources of any kind. You can verify that by viewing the page source — the only outbound links point to GitHub.

Why, and on what basis

Your address is used for one purpose: to send you a message when Pomnia is released, and occasional updates about the project afterwards. The lawful basis is your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, given by submitting the form and confirming it through the link we email you. Nothing happens until you click that link.

Your address is never used for profiling, never sold, never rented, and never passed to advertisers.

Who processes it

The list is stored and delivered by MailerLite (UAB "MailerLite", Vilnius, Lithuania), acting as a data processor under a data processing agreement. They are inside the EU, so your data does not leave the European Economic Area.

Email sent to hello@pomnia.ai is handled by my hosting provider, cyber_Folks S.A. (Poland).

How long it is kept

Until you unsubscribe. There is an unsubscribe link at the bottom of every message, and using it removes your address from the list. You can also just email me and I will delete it.

Your rights

Under GDPR you can, at any time:

  • ask what data I hold about you, and get a copy of it
  • have it corrected if it is wrong
  • have it deleted
  • withdraw your consent, which has the same effect as unsubscribing
  • object to the processing, or ask for it to be restricted

Write to hello@pomnia.ai and I will act on it. If you think I have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

About the Pomnia application

This page covers the website only. The Pomnia application itself stores your vault on your own machine or on a server you run. Its data handling is documented with the application, and no vault content is ever transmitted to me.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, I will say so in an email to the list rather than quietly editing this page. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
